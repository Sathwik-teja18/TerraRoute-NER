How the queue works
P1 Emergency cargo is processed first. P2 Essential cargo is processed next. P3 Standard cargo follows. When shipments have equal priority, the earlier registered shipment is processed first using First Come First Served (FCFS).
Cargo Priority Classification
Explainable priority assignment

P1 — Emergency

Medical supplies, emergency relief materials, disaster-response cargo and other critical time-sensitive shipments.

P2 — Essential

Food supplies, fuel, infrastructure materials and other essential goods.

P3 — Standard

Furniture, general goods and non-critical commercial shipments.
